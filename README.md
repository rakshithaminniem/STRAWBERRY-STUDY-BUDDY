# 🍓 Strawberry Study Buddy

![Strawberry Study Buddy](Screenshot.png)

## What is this?
I was supposed to study.

Instead, I made a study website.😗

Strawberry Study Buddy is a small web project I made to keep study sessions a little more organized without making the whole thing feel too serious.

It brings together a study timer, daily goals, a checklist, and subjects — all inside one cute strawberry-themed website.

Basically:

**📚 Pick a subject → 🎯 Set your goals → ⏰ Start studying → ✅ Check things off → 🍓 Feel productive**

## 🎮 How to use it

1. Open the website.
2. Enter your username and password.
3. 🍓 Enter your study space.
4. 📚 Add the subjects you're studying.
5. 🎯 Set your goals for the day.
6. ✅ Add tasks to your checklist.
7. ⏰ Start a 25-minute or 45-minute study session.
8. 📖 Study.
9. ✨ Check off what you completed.
10. 🍓 Repeat until you feel productive. 😭
